CHAD

  • Capital: N'Djamena.
  • Official Languages: French and Arabic.
  • Geography: A shallow basin rising to mountains, with Lake Chad in the west, the Chari and Logone rivers, and desert in the north.
  • Climate: Varies from arid in the north (Sahara) to tropical in the south, with distinct wet/dry seasons.
  • Humanitarian Crisis: Faces high levels of hunger, poverty, and food insecurity, worsened by climate change and conflicts in neighboring Sudan.

  • Development: One of the world's least developed nations, relying heavily on agriculture but facing resource pressures and poverty.
